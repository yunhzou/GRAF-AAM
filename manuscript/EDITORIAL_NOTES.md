## Molecular figure conventions

Use color on the actual chemical features: translucent red highlights for cleavage/weakening and translucent blue highlights for formation/strengthening, drawn behind the original solid black bond lines. Preserve single/double bond strokes so existing connectivity stays visible; never replace those strokes with dashed or opaque event-color lines. Retain consistent pastel fragment colors across mapped endpoints, with atom identities explicitly traceable. Different candidate partitions may assign colors differently and must say so. Prefer a real saved Golden or coordinate example for discussion figures. Count and color events using the original evaluation matrices, independently of conventional bond orders used for depiction. Keep benchmark outputs distinct from chemical pathway claims.
